# BudgetApp
Budget and finance tool with added productivity tools. Installable, works offline, and keeps all data on your device.

## Features
- **Home dashboard** – left to spend, safe-to-spend per day, insights and warnings, upcoming bills, budgets, goals, tasks due
- **Activity** – every transaction by day, search, filters (expenses, income, recurring, with notes), all-months search
- **Full transaction pages** – edit name, amount, category, date, note, attach photo/PDF receipts, duplicate, make monthly, skip a month, stop repeating
- **Budget** – category limits, donut breakdown, custom categories (name, emoji, colour), category pages with 6-month trend
- **Bills** – fixed and varying bills, due days, mark as paid, calendar view, recurring income, yearly cost of bills
- **Savings & goals** – savings balance, goals with target dates and monthly plans, net position
- **Debts** – payoff dates, interest, avalanche vs snowball comparison, payment log
- **Shopping lists** – quantities, "can I afford it?", savings plan, turn into a goal, log purchases into your budget
- **To-do** – due dates, priorities, notes, grouped by overdue / today / upcoming
- **Reports & history** – income vs spending, category trends, month comparisons, biggest expenses, month detail pages
- **Settings** – currency, light/dark theme, backup & restore (with receipts), CSV export, bank CSV import with auto-categorising

Your existing data from the previous version loads automatically.
